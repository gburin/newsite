The most recent paper that I co-authored is out now and was featured on the [cover of its issue](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/toc/14698137/2025/248/6) at New Phytologist. In this paper, Diego was interested in assessing the effects of a floral morphological syndrome on the diversification of an important group of Neotropical seed plants. [The paper is available in open access](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nph.70623), so go check it out!

On a very nice sidenote, this paper is quite important to me since Diego was a student in the grad course in Macroevolution that I co-taught during the lockdown. This led him to invite me to be part of his qualification committee, and eventually the collaboration grew enough for me to become co-author in the paper.
